# MolFlood Community Models

MolFlood is intended to support a curated collection of target-specific models for neglected and understudied diseases.

The future Community Model Standard is expected to require MolFlood version, contributor information, disease/target metadata, docking provenance, structural validation, applicability-domain information, dataset/model hashes, environment metadata, licensing, and redistribution rights.

Unreviewed serialized model files must not be automatically loaded in trusted infrastructure.
